How to play

  1. Deal in. Press Enter on New Run, then again to start the first blind.
  2. Build a hand. Move with the arrow keys. Press Z to select or deselect up to five cards. Gold cards are selected.
  3. Check the preview. The bottom of the game shows your hand and its chips × multiplier score.
  4. Play or redraw. Press E to score selected cards, or Q to discard them. You get four hands and four discards each blind.
  5. Buy a boost. Beat the target, then press Enter to visit the shop. Select an offer and press Z to buy. Press X to continue.
  6. Beat the boss. Reach 300, then 450, then 600 points. The final blind reduces your hand to seven cards.
